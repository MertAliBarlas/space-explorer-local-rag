# Mars Rovers
Mars rovers are automated motor vehicles that propel themselves across the surface of the planet Mars upon arrival. Prominent rovers include Sojourner, Spirit, Opportunity, Curiosity, and Perseverance. The main objective of the Mars Perseverance Rover is to seek signs of ancient life and collect samples of rock and regolith for possible return to Earth.
