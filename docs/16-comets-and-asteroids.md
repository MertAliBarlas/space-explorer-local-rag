# Comets and Asteroids
Asteroids are rocky, airless worlds that orbit our Sun. Comets are cosmic snowballs of frozen gases, rock, and dust that orbit the Sun. When a comet's orbit brings it close to the Sun, it heats up and spews dust and gases into a giant glowing head larger than most planets.
