# Neutron Stars vs White Dwarfs
A neutron star is the collapsed core of a massive supergiant star. They are the smallest and densest currently known class of stellar objects. In contrast, a white dwarf is what stars like the Sun become after they have exhausted their nuclear fuel. Neutron stars are much denser and smaller than white dwarfs.
