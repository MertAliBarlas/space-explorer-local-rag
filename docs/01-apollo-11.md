# Apollo 11
Apollo 11 was the American spaceflight that first landed humans on the Moon. Commander Neil Armstrong and lunar module pilot Buzz Aldrin formed the American crew that landed the Apollo Lunar Module Eagle on July 20, 1969. The mission was a major milestone in the Space Race, successfully fulfilling President John F. Kennedy's goal of landing a man on the moon by the end of the decade.
