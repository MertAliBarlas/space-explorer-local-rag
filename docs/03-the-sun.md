# The Sun
The Sun is the star at the center of the Solar System. It is a nearly perfect sphere of hot plasma, heated to incandescence by nuclear fusion reactions in its core. The Sun is about 93 million miles (150 million kilometers) away from Earth. Solar flares, which are sudden flashes of increased brightness on the Sun, can affect satellite communications and power grids on Earth.
